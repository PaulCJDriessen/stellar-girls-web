Specialty bars, Encore, Synch Pulse

Match-3 feel

Gem colors now feed specialty bars with a real payoff at full: a once-per-stage burst tied to that specialty. Clear a stage early and Encore keeps your leftover moves so the win can still breathe — or End Performance when you’re done.

Synch Pulse teaches Chemistry on the board without turning Living Group into a spreadsheet. Shine stays its own meter; the bars are about performance meaning, not replacing center skills.
